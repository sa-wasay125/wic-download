Windows

Windows 10 / 11 laptops & PCs
  1. Download and open WIC-Setup.exe.

  3. Click Next until finished.

Android

Phones & tablets, Android 6+
  1. Download WIC-Android.apk and tap it.
  2. If asked, allow installing from this source (once only).
  3. Tap Install.
Download for Android

Mac

macOS 11 or newer
  1. Download WIC-Mac.zip; it unzips to WIC. Drag it to Applications.
  2. Open it. If macOS blocks it: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (once).
Download for Mac

iPhone / iPad DEMO

Demo install, valid 7 days
  1. On a Windows PC or Mac, install the free tool Sideloadly (sideloadly.io).
  2. Connect the iPhone by USB, drag WIC-iPhone.ipa into Sideloadly, sign in with your Apple ID, click Start.
  3. On the iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → trust your Apple ID.
  4. Repeat every 7 days.
Download iPhone file

Connecting the camera

1. Switch the camera on (hold power 3 seconds until the blue light is on).
2. On your device, join the camera's Wi-Fi network (name and password are in the camera's manual).
3. Open WIC and tap Connect. Press the camera's button to take a photo.
